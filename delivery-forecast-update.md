# Delivery forecast update template

Free to copy into Confluence, Jira, Word or any wiki. Replace everything in [brackets].
Online version with guidance: https://clearlaneapps.com/guides/delivery-forecast-update-template/

## Delivery forecast: [release or epic], week of [date]

## 1. In one sentence

[Likely by [date]; very likely by [date]. Changed since last week because [reason].]

## 2. Dates

| Confidence | Date | Last week |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 50% (likely) | [date] | [date] |
| 85% (safe to promise) | [date] | [date] |

## 3. Inputs

Work items left: [number] (scope added this week: [number]). Items finished per week over the last [6] weeks: [numbers]. Method: [Monte Carlo on throughput / other].

## 4. Risks

- [Risk, impact on the dates, owner.]

## 5. Decision needed

[Cut scope / accept the date / add people], by [name], before [date].
