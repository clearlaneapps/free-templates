# Free templates for Jira and Confluence teams

Two free calculators go with them:

- [CRA Article 14 deadline calculator](https://clearlaneapps.com/guides/cra-article-14-deadline-calculator/): early warning, notification and final report dates under the Cyber Resilience Act.
- [NIS2 incident reporting deadline calculator](https://clearlaneapps.com/guides/nis2-incident-reporting-deadline-calculator/): early warning, incident notification and final report dates under NIS2 Article 23.

These templates help you structure the work. They are not legal advice, and using them does not by itself make you meet any regulation.
